# Comparative Macroeconomic Analysis: Switzerland vs Latvia (2012–2023)

Individual university project (Macroeconomics, Universitat Jaume I, 2025).

**Files**
- `report.pdf` — written analysis (4 pages)
- `macro_switzerland_latvia.xlsx` — data and calculations (Eurostat / Penn World Table data)

**What it covers**
1. Nominal vs real GDP growth, CPI vs GDP deflator, and the unemployment–GDP relationship around COVID-19 (2020–2021).
2. GDP composition, volatility of each component (standard deviation of annual growth) and trade openness.
3. Solow-Swan model test (2017): capital per worker, population growth, human capital and institutional quality vs output per worker.

**Main findings**
- Latvia grew faster than Switzerland in both nominal and real terms, with higher inflation.
- Latvia is the more open economy (trade openness peaking at 158% of GDP in 2022 vs 111–124% for Switzerland).
- Capital per worker explains part of Switzerland's productivity lead, but the population-growth prediction of Solow-Swan does not hold here: human capital and institutional quality appear to matter more.
